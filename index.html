Mock Mode

Start the app from its folder with node server.js or use Start Budget App.vbs. Enter your Plaid credentials, select the matching environment, save, and connect an institution. Use Add Institution to connect more banks; their accounts and transactions are combined here. After you connect, the server saves the long-lived connection and credentials in a vault encrypted for your Windows account, so the institution normally returns after a restart. A bank may still ask you to re-authenticate if it requires renewed consent or your login changes.
